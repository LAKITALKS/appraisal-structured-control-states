This document explains, for outside readers, exactly where the project stands.

## Completed at the preregistration stage

- **Prior-art boundary** — verified against primary sources; the shared-family
  claim, not any single axis, is the defensible novelty.
- **Family-structure test** — family claim = **A ∧ B ∧ C** (all required): a shared
  low-rank model vs separate models (pooled held-out log-loss, nested CV, rank grid
  `{1,2}`), structured behavioral specificity, and incremental shared contribution.
- **Hypotheses H1/H2/H3/H4** — structure unchanged from v0.1.0; H4 exploratory.
- **Direction derivation** — one fixed primary rule (regularized logistic
  regression; normalized difference-in-means intervention direction).
- **Stimulus QA** — typed, two-mode (`draft` / `run_ready`) protocol.
- **Axis isolation** — one primary axis per matched group; multi-axis items are a
  separate secondary robustness set.
- **Response-strategy taxonomy** — nine fine labels plus four primary superclasses,
  with a deterministic dominance/tie-breaker rule.
- **Mini-shard protocol** — feasibility studies with immutable run manifests.
- **Run-ready gates** — code-enforced before any activation extraction.
- **External-data provenance** — no unreviewed external labels; unknown
  training-data overlap called *unknown*.
- **Baselines** — prompt-embedding and difficulty-representation as primary
  comparisons.
- **Seeds and determinism** — temperature-0 primary generation; seeds are not
  independent text samples.
- **Non-claims** — unchanged.
- **Holonomy exclusion** — ASCR mini-shards collect no holonomy, dialogue-loop,
  path-dependence, or user-signature data.
